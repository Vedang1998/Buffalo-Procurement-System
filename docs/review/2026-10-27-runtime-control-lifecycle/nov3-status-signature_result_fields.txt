436: *** GOODSIG  <long_keyid_or_fpr>  <username>
437:     The signature with the keyid is good.  For each signature only one
438:     of the codes GOODSIG, BADSIG, EXPSIG, EXPKEYSIG, REVKEYSIG or
439:     ERRSIG will be emitted.  In the past they were used as a marker
440:     for a new signature; new code should use the NEWSIG status
441:     instead.  The username is the primary one encoded in UTF-8 and %XX
442:     escaped. The fingerprint may be used instead of the long keyid if
443:     it is available.  This is the case with CMS and might eventually
444:     also be available for OpenPGP.
445: 
446: *** EXPSIG  <long_keyid_or_fpr>  <username>
447:     The signature with the keyid is good, but the signature is
448:     expired. The username is the primary one encoded in UTF-8 and %XX
449:     escaped. The fingerprint may be used instead of the long keyid if
450:     it is available.  This is the case with CMS and might eventually
451:     also be available for OpenPGP.
452: 
453: *** EXPKEYSIG  <long_keyid_or_fpr> <username>
454:     The signature with the keyid is good, but the signature was made
455:     by an expired key. The username is the primary one encoded in
456:     UTF-8 and %XX escaped.  The fingerprint may be used instead of the
457:     long keyid if it is available.  This is the case with CMS and
458:     might eventually also be available for OpenPGP.
459: 
460: *** REVKEYSIG  <long_keyid_or_fpr>  <username>
461:     The signature with the keyid is good, but the signature was made
462:     by a revoked key. The username is the primary one encoded in UTF-8
463:     and %XX escaped. The fingerprint may be used instead of the long
464:     keyid if it is available.  This is the case with CMS and might
465:     eventually also beñ available for OpenPGP.
466: 
467: *** BADSIG  <long_keyid_or_fpr>  <username>
468:     The signature with the keyid has not been verified okay.  The
469:     username is the primary one encoded in UTF-8 and %XX escaped. The
470:     fingerprint may be used instead of the long keyid if it is
471:     available.  This is the case with CMS and might eventually also be
472:     available for OpenPGP.
473: 
474: *** ERRSIG  <keyid>  <pkalgo> <hashalgo> <sig_class> <time> <rc> <fpr>
475:     It was not possible to check the signature.  This may be caused by
476:     a missing public key or an unsupported algorithm.  A RC of 4
477:     indicates unknown algorithm, a 9 indicates a missing public
478:     key. The other fields give more information about this signature.
479:     sig_class is a 2 byte hex-value.  The fingerprint may be used
480:     instead of the long_keyid_or_fpr if it is available.  This is the
481:     case with gpgsm and might eventually also be available for
482:     OpenPGP.  The ERRSIG line has FPR filed which is only available
483:     since 2.2.7; that FPR may either be missing or - if the signature
484:     has no fingerprint as meta data.
485: 
486:     Note, that TIME may either be the number of seconds since Epoch or
487:     an ISO 8601 string.  The latter can be detected by the presence of
488:     the letter 'T'.
489: 
490: *** VALIDSIG <args>
491: 
492:     The args are:
493: 
494:     - <fingerprint_in_hex>
495:     - <sig_creation_date>
496:     - <sig-timestamp>
497:     - <expire-timestamp>
498:     - <sig-version>
499:     - <reserved>
500:     - <pubkey-algo>
501:     - <hash-algo>
502:     - <sig-class>
503:     - [ <primary-key-fpr> ]
504: 
505:     This status indicates that the signature is cryptographically
506:     valid. This is similar to GOODSIG, EXPSIG, EXPKEYSIG, or REVKEYSIG
507:     (depending on the date and the state of the signature and signing
508:     key) but has the fingerprint as the argument. Multiple status
509:     lines (VALIDSIG and the other appropriate *SIG status) are emitted
510:     for a valid signature.  All arguments here are on one long line.
511:     sig-timestamp is the signature creation time in seconds after the
512:     epoch. expire-timestamp is the signature expiration time in
513:     seconds after the epoch (zero means "does not
514:     expire"). sig-version, pubkey-algo, hash-algo, and sig-class (a
515:     2-byte hex value) are all straight from the signature packet.
516:     PRIMARY-KEY-FPR is the fingerprint of the primary key or identical
517:     to the first argument.  This is useful to get back to the primary
518:     key without running gpg again for this purpose.
519: 
520:     The primary-key-fpr parameter is used for OpenPGP and not
521:     available for CMS signatures.  The sig-version as well as the sig
522:     class is not defined for CMS and currently set to 0 and 00.
523:
